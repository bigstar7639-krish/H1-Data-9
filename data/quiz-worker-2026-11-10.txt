Q+A Katha - Love 🧠

Question: Which Pokemon is #39 in the National Pokedex?

Answer: Jigglypuff - Entertainment: Board Games. Itha kelvi Love pathi - Answer Jigglypuff. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Love pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Love la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Love oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - God 🧠

Question: Who is the bassist of the British rock band Queen?

Answer: John Deacon - Entertainment: Music. Itha kelvi God pathi - Answer John Deacon. Oru kaalathula oru oor la God nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan God oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan God pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam God ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye God nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, God ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
